Blackbyrd3D and your privacy

Blackbyrd3D has no telemetry, analytics or crash reporting. Your models stay on your computer.

Update check (on unless you turn it off)
Each time Blackbyrd3D starts, it asks api.github.com whether a newer release exists. The request carries no information about you or your work, but GitHub sees your IP address (GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
To turn it off, clear "Check GitHub for updates on startup" on the next pages. You can change it later in Settings > Updates.

AI assistant (off until you set it up)
If you connect a provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, a ChatGPT account through Codex, or a local or custom server) and press Send, your prompts and a description of the scene are sent to that provider, and a viewport screenshot when the assistant asks for one. Nothing is sent until you do this. That provider's privacy policy applies.

Local connections
Blackbyrd3D listens on 127.0.0.1 (this computer only) so scripts and assistants you run can drive it. Set BYRD3D_NO_RPC=1 to turn this off.

Settings and API keys are stored in plain text in %USERPROFILE%\.config\blackbyrd3d. Uninstalling Blackbyrd3D leaves them in place; delete that folder to remove them.

Full policy: https://github.com/JonasPedrotti/Blackbyrd3D/blob/main/docs/privacy.md
